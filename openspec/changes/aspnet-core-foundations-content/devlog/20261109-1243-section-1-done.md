## Gotcha

Starlight's aside icon `<svg>` has no `aria-hidden` of its own. The hidden attribute is on its parent,
`<p class="starlight-aside__title" aria-hidden="true">`. That's why the SVG test walks up the
ancestors. Without that, every page with an aside would fail.

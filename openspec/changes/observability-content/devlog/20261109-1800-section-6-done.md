## Decision to flag to Emmz: a bounded `payment.method`

The plan didn't call for this. `payment.method` comes from the client's request, so used raw as a
tag it's unbounded: the very cardinality trap the exercise teaches. So I added
`OrderMetrics.PaymentMethodTag`, which maps to `card`, `voucher` or `other`, in **both** Solution and
Starter (provided, no new TODO), plus a fourth test, `UnknownPaymentMethodsShareOneTagValue`. The
spec and D5 are still met. It's small to remove if she'd rather not have it. **For the Metrics
page:** a good "bounded enums" example, from the exercise.

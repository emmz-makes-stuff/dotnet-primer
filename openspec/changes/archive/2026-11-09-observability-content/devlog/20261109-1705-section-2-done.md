throws (`@fail.test`), but 04 let `PaymentGatewayException` escape as a 500. Emmz chose "catch, 502,
log Error":

  "failed", "the payment gateway is unavailable")`. **05 and 06 should use the same response;**

  `Exception` parameter) is in the Solution **and, already written, in the Starter**. The Starter

  to ("like PaymentFailed below"). No new TODO;

  and checks both emails.

## Gotchas found

- **`dotnet run` applies `Properties/launchSettings.json`, whose `applicationUrl` overrides
  `ASPNETCORE_URLS`.** The app listened on 5291, not 5091. Use `--no-launch-profile`, or the
  profile's port. This is worth a pitfall, or at least a note, wherever a page sets the URL or OTEL
  env vars for `dotnet run`. The launch profile can also carry `OTEL_*` variables.
- **Verification harness:** Node `spawnSync` blocks the event loop, so a background load timer
  doesn't fire. Use async `spawn`. The scripts are in the scratchpad: `probe-run.mjs` and
  `counters-run.mjs`.
- The container is removed: `docker ps --filter name=aspire-dashboard` shows 0.

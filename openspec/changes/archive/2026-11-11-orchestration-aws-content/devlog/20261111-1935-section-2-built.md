## 2.4: the Starter

The Starter's `Orders.cs` is identical to the Solution's. Its `Program.cs` differs only at the four
TODO sites:
1. `app.MapHealthChecks("/health/live")`, which runs every check;
2. no `/health/ready`, and the store's check untagged;
3. no shutting-down check and no drain delay (`Health.cs` has the TODO 3 hint);
4. `ShutdownTimeout` 1 s, with "Keep shutdown short, so deploys are quick."

**Against the Starter:** 4 of 5 fail, each with its guiding message, and `PlacedOrderCanBeFetched`
passes:
- `LivenessIgnoresTheStore`: `GET /health/live returned 503 ServiceUnavailable, expected 200 OK:
  with the store offline, liveness failed. … Liveness should run no checks (TODO 1).`
- `ReadinessFollowsTheStore`: `GET /health/ready returned 404: there's no readiness endpoint. …
  (TODO 2).`
- `ReadinessFailsWhileShuttingDown`: `GET /health/ready returned 404, so there's no readiness to
  fail during shutdown. Do TODO 2 first, then TODO 3.`
- `InFlightOrderCompletesOnShutdown`: `the order in flight was cut off when the app stopped: its
  connection was dropped. … (TODO 4).`

**A change from `1920`:** TODO 3's test now **fails** on a missing `/health/ready` instead of
skipping. The spec's "Starter's health checks are wrong" scenario says the health tests *fail*
against the starter, each naming the TODO that fixes it, and the spec beats 2.4's wording. So
leaving out TODO 2 fails TODO 3's test too, with the message "Do TODO 2 first". That's the one
overlap.

**A bug that the copies found, now fixed in the Solution:** without TODO 4, *every* test failed.
The 1 s shutdown timeout cancelled the drain delay's `Task.Delay`, and `StoppingAsync` threw
`TaskCanceledException` out of `Host.StopAsync`, which `WebApplicationFactory.Dispose` surfaced.
In production, that means the app exits with an error. `DrainDelay.StoppingAsync` now catches
`OperationCanceledException` when its token is cancelled, logs a warning ("The shutdown timeout ran
out during the drain delay") and returns. The Starter's TODO 3 hint says to do the same. **This
belongs on the page**, as a Pitfall or a line in the drain-delay section.

After the fix, `Solution.Tests` passed **ten runs in a row** again (10.32–10.43 s).
